Finding a planet in a star's light

Astronomers often find planets they can't see. When a planet passes in front of its star, the star gets a little dimmer. Change the planet's size and watch the dip.

Investigate

1. Make the planet twice as wide. Does the dip get twice as deep? Write down your prediction first.
2. Find the smallest planet whose dip you could still notice on a noisy graph.
3. Why might it be easier to find big planets than small ones?

Note: this graph is a simplified model for learning, not real telescope data. Real data lessons are planned.
